How donor registration works

Three simple steps to join the HemoSync donor community.

01 / REGISTER

Create your donor profile

Enter your basic details, blood group and preferred contact information through the donor registration form.

02 / UPDATE

Manage your availability

Keep your contact details and donation availability up to date. You can choose whether you are currently available.

03 / HELP

Respond when you can

If an appropriate opportunity is shared with you, decide whether you are willing and able to donate. Participation is always voluntary.

Your trust matters

Your information deserves careful protection.

HemoSync supports donor registration and coordination. Donor information should be handled responsibly, shared only with authorized people and used for the purpose explained during registration.

🔐
Private donor detailsYour personal information should only be accessible to your account and authorized staff.
🛡️
Consent-led communicationYour contact details and availability should not be publicly exposed or used without permission.
✓
Donation is always voluntaryDonors decide whether they are willing and eligible. Medical suitability must be assessed by qualified professionals.
